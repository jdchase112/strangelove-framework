The Shining

1980
Stanley Kubrick

A family heads to an isolated hotel for the winter, where a malevolent presence influences the father into violence. At the same time, his psychic son sees horrifying forebodings from both the past and the future.

Perfect Blue

1997
Satoshi Kon

Rising pop star Mima gives up her career to become an actress, but she slowly goes insane when she starts being stalked by an obsessed fan and what seems to be a ghost of her past.

David Lynch
